Technical Dossier

Engineering & Coachwork

Cylinder Capacity 7.7-Litre (7,668 cc) Crossflow OHV Inline-6 with dual spark ignition.
Transmission 4-speed manual gearbox with synchromesh on 3rd and top gear.
Chassis 144-inch short wheelbase Continental frame with underslung rear springs.
Braking System Mechanical friction servo operating on all four drum brakes.
Hand-beaten aluminium panels formed over seasoned English ash framing. The waistline features Barker’s hallmark sweeping streamline molding, raked windscreen, Lucas P100 headlamps, and twin side-mounted spares.
Adorned with original Vaumol navy blue leather hides and deep-pile Wilton carpets. Rear compartment conceals twin fold-out burr walnut tables complete with solid sterling silver capped crystal decanters.
