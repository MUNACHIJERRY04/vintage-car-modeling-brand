Section 8.1 Specification Disclosures

Comprehensive Technical Dossier & Lineage

Engineered under the direct supervision of Sir Henry Royce, the Continental chassis was conceived for high-speed grand touring across the open motorways of Continental Europe.

Engine Architecture 7.7-Litre (7,668 cc) Inline-6

Crossflow head, pushrod overhead valves, twin ignition via coil and high-tension magneto.

Bore & Stroke Ratio 107.9 mm × 139.7 mm

High-compression Continental cylinder head (5.25:1) with dual sparking plugs per cylinder.

Induction & Carburation Dual-Jet Rolls-Royce Automatic

Automatic expanding carburettor featuring secondary starting jet and electrical pre-heating.

Transmission Unit 4-Speed Manual Synchromesh

Right-hand gate-shift lever, synchromesh on 3rd and 4th gears, unit-mounted clutch assembly.

Chassis & Underslung Rear Short 144″ Wheelbase Frame

Low-slung Continental channel-section chassis, semi-elliptic underslung rear leaf springs.

Mechanical Servo Braking Hispano-Licensed Friction Servo

Gearbox-driven mechanical servo applying proportional braking effort to all four 15″ drums.

speed Continuous High-Speed Rating: Tested 92.3 mph sustained run at Montlhéry Autodrome.
Derby Works Record Verified

Aluminum-over-Ash Architecture

Constructed by King George V’s official coachbuilders, Barker & Co. Ltd., this bespoke Sports Saloon features a raked windscreen, channeled close-coupled body, and aluminum outer skins shaped entirely by hand over seasoned English ash framing. The waistline features Barker’s signature streamline molding dipping gracefully before the rear quarters.

Luggage Trunk Integration Lucas P100 Headlamps Concealed Spare Carrier

Mayfair Cabin Furnishings

The interior is appointed with original Vaumol dark navy leather hides, accented by deep-pile Wilton wool carpeting dyed to coordinate with the midnight exterior. The rear passenger compartment conceals twin fold-out burr walnut cocktail and vanity tables complete with solid sterling silver capped cut-crystal flasks engraved with the original owner’s family armorial.
